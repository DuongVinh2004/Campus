# Campus 24/7 — Campus Studio
## Purpose
A calm, capable university workspace. Help students find a service, prepare a request, understand its status, and get grounded assistance. Staff workspaces prioritize queues, review, and accountable decisions.
This is a new visual direction. Existing business behavior and security boundaries remain authoritative.

## Visual identity
- Canvas: warm paper #F6F7F9. Surfaces: #FFFFFF. Ink: #182C3A.
- Brand: deep teal #155E63, hover #104C50. Brand tint: #EAF3F1.
- Accent: ochre #D6A34A, pale sand #F7EEDC. Use sparingly for illustration.
- Secondary text: #5D6D78. Borders: #E1E7EB.
- Success, warning and error always include text or an icon; color alone carries no meaning.
- Dark: canvas #101B22, surface #172731, text #EAF0F3, brand #9ED8CF.
- Typography: existing Inter with Vietnamese coverage; 15px body, 1.65 line-height, restrained 500–650 weights.
- Display: 40–48px desktop, 30–34px mobile; natural Vietnamese wrapping, no forced clipping.
- Spacing: 4px base; 8/12/16/24/32/40/48. Cards 18px radius, inputs/buttons 10px.
- Elevation: borders and restrained shadows. No acrylic panels, neon gradients, glowing blobs or animated status claims.

## Layout and navigation
- Desktop: 244px left navigation, 76px top toolbar, content maximum 1320px.
- Mobile below 960px: compact toolbar, accessible modal navigation, content padding 20px.
- Primary navigation: overview, assistant, services, requests, schedule, rooms; support and personal settings grouped separately.
- Staff/admin navigation retains role filtering and existing environment-specific destinations.
- No duplicated global navigation inside content. Page header gives title, purpose, and one main action.
- Dashboard: greeting, assistant invitation, four practical service shortcuts, schedule and request summaries.
- Empty states explain what is missing and offer a relevant next step. Never manufacture academic records.

## Components
- Primary actions: deep teal, white text. Secondary: white/surface with border. Destructive actions retain explicit labels and confirmation.
- Forms: visible label, helpful description, error tied to its field, preserved draft, clear preview before commit.
- Lists/tables: readable row spacing, aligned columns, selected row state; horizontal scrolling only inside the table.
- Chat: quiet canvas, prominent composer, useful prompt cards, accessible history drawer, source references near answers.
- Loading: stable skeleton or concise status. Connection errors remain honest and recoverable.
- All buttons and navigation targets are at least 44px in their primary dimension.
- Focus: visible 2px teal outline with offset. Dialogs trap/restore focus and close with Escape.
- Motion: 160–220ms opacity/color. Respect reduced motion; never animate essential content indefinitely.

## Validation
Check main routes at 1440, 1024, 768 and 390px; keyboard navigation; menu dismissal and focus restoration; light/dark themes; long Vietnamese text; errors and empty states. Browser screenshots support visual review, not an assertion of usability or complete accessibility compliance.
